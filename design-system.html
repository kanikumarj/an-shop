Motion Principles
🎯 Purposeful
Every animation communicates state change. Motion is never decorative alone — it guides attention and confirms actions.
⚡ Responsive
Interactions feel instant (≤100ms feedback). Never make the user wait without visual indication of progress.
🌊 Natural
Physics-based spring easing for UI elements. Avoid linear animations — they feel mechanical and cheap.
✨ Cinematic
Hero sections, loading screens and transitions use longer durations with layered staggered reveals for a premium feel.
