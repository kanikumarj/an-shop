Our Homemade Murukku is a traditional South Indian delicacy crafted in small batches using time-honoured family recipes. Made from the finest rice flour and urad dal, each spiral is pressed by hand — giving it that authentic, irregular texture that sets handmade murukku apart from factory-made versions.

We season every batch with roasted whole sesame seeds, freshly ground cumin, and just the right pinch of asafoetida — ingredients chosen not just for flavour, but for their digestive benefits rooted in Ayurvedic tradition. The result is a snack that is crispy without being oily, flavourful without being overpowering.

Fried to perfection in refined oil at precisely controlled temperatures, our murukku achieves that satisfying crunch in every bite. It stays fresh for 30 days when stored in an airtight container — though we've yet to meet a family that hasn't finished it long before that!
